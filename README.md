A tiny scorer that picks the funniest real-life patch note from six candidates and writes the winner to /output/entry.txt.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

## The pick

The winner, scored 82 points against the judge's published criteria:

> v3.2.1: fixed a bug where the brain replayed your most embarrassing moment from 2014 every night at 3 a.m. instead of sleeping.

Why it wins: it is a real release note in form (version number, "fixed a bug where"), it names a specific and instantly recognisable bug of human biology (the 3 a.m. cringe replay), it carries two concrete details (the year 2014, the clock time 3 a.m.) that make the scene land on first read, and at 127 characters it uses its space without padding. The runners-up score lower because they carry fewer concrete details: the earworm song (59), the bladder's low-battery warning (58), the legs falling asleep (55), the contagious meeting yawn (54), and the public sneeze (49).
